Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Unchained Label (“Unchained”, “we”, “us”) collects through unchainedlabel.com, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: shop@unchainedlabel.com.

1. What we collect and why

When you…We collectWhy
Join the mailing list (“Inside Access”)Your email addressTo send you pre-sale tickets, drops, restocks and your welcome discount code. Based on your consent; unsubscribe any time.
Use the contact or demo-submission formsName, email, message and any links or files you includeTo reply to you and review demos. Based on your request / our legitimate interest in answering you.
Buy merchandiseName, address, email, order and payment details, entered on the Shopify checkoutTo process and deliver your order. We never see or store your full card number.

Accept analytics & ad cookiesOnline identifiers and browsing activity such as page views, add-to-cart and checkout eventsTo measure the site and our advertising. Based on your consent where required.

We do not sell your personal information.

2. Cookies and similar storage

We keep this to a minimum and we don’t show a full-screen cookie wall.

Strictly necessary / functional (no consent needed)

These are stored in your browser’s local storage and are never used to track you across other sites:

  • unchained_consent — remembers your cookie choice.
  • unchained_cart_id, unchained_checkout_url — keep your shopping bag between pages.
  • unchained_site_data, unchained_last_fetch — a cached copy of our events/releases so pages load fast.
  • unchained_popup_signup — stops the sign-up prompt appearing again after you join.

Analytics, advertising and chat (consent)

  • Meta Pixel (home and shop pages) — sets cookies such as _fbp to measure ads and visits on Facebook/Instagram.
  • Google Tag Manager (home and shop pages) — loads measurement tags and may set cookies such as _ga.
  • Crisp (all pages) — the live-chat widget stores a session cookie so your conversation continues.

If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland or Indonesia, these stay switched off until you press Accept on the small card at the bottom of the page. Elsewhere they load by default and you can turn them off at any time. Either way you can change your mind whenever you like:

Open cookie settings

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Embedded content

The Label page embeds a Spotify player, and our pages load fonts and icons from third-party content networks (Font Awesome via cdnjs). Those providers can see your IP address when they serve those files, and Spotify may set its own cookies. Their own policies apply.

3. Who we share information with

We use these service providers (“processors”) to run the site. They only get what they need:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, security and delivery.
  • Shopify — store, checkout and order management.
  • Megatix — event ticketing (embedded checkout).
  • MailerLite — mailing list and email delivery.
  • Formspree — delivers contact and demo form submissions to us.
  • Crisp — live chat.
  • Meta Platforms and Google — advertising and analytics (only with consent where required).
  • Google Sheets — hosts the public event, release and artist listings your browser loads.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and safety.

4. International transfers

Our providers operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, the EU and Singapore. Where required we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the provider’s own transfer mechanisms.

5. How long we keep it

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe.
  • Contact messages and demos: as long as needed to deal with your request, then deleted within a reasonable time.
  • Order records: as long as required for tax and accounting law.
  • Consent choice: stored in your browser until you clear it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law or Australia’s Privacy Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, receive a copy of it, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn’t affect anything done before you withdrew.

To use these rights email shop@unchainedlabel.com. To leave the mailing list, use the unsubscribe link in any email. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The site is not directed to children under 16 and we don’t knowingly collect their information. Some events have their own age limits set by the venue and ticketing platform.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, restrict who can access our systems and keep API credentials on the server side. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
